## Description

The product owner, 2026-10-05/06: FlexDesk owns table display and the whole Excel-like grid, editing included
(docs/05 C9's original design in Tables). A consumer that re-implements one of these features is the defect.

Shipped so far: 0.4.7 (C37, a page's own levels), 0.5.0 (list workarounds retired), 0.6.0 (columns over objects,
the first row furniture), 0.6.1–0.6.3 (DataTable diff, scroll lock, paging / `resolvePaging`, blank-rows fix) and
0.7.0 (groups, trees, server-driven rows, the three adoption fixes). What remains is STORY-0002 (table display) and
STORY-0003 (the editable grid), plus STORY-0004.

Tables' side (pinning each release and deleting its own copies) stays in Tables' backlog: STORY-0210, STORY-0216,
STORY-0217.
