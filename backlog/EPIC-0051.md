## Description

FlexDesk shows progress only as a spinner and a message, so apps with parallel or multi-phase jobs build their own bars and their own toast layers next to NotificationCenter. This adds domain-free progress primitives and progress-carrying notifications.

Filed from the EcoSim -> FlexDesk 0.16 migration analysis (2026-10-17).
