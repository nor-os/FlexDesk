## Description

_canonicalize (src/tiling/wm.js:3184-3200) detaches panel leaves and collapses their split; wrap() (3224-3254) builds a new split with hard-coded sizes [1,4]/[4,1]. Called from load (247), switchDesktop (3289), togglePanel (3147) and other paths.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Drag the Navigator to ~40% width and the Console to ~50% height.
2. Reload, switch desktop and back, or toggle the right panel.

## Expected
User sizes are kept.

## Actual
All open panels return to 1:4.
