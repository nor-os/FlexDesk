## Description

_canonicalize (src/tiling/wm.js:3184-3200) detaches panel leaves and collapses their split; wrap() (3224-3254) builds a new split with hard-coded sizes [1,4]/[4,1]. Called from load (247), switchDesktop (3289), togglePanel (3147) and other paths.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Drag the Navigator to ~40% width and the Console to ~50% height.
2. Reload, switch desktop and back, or toggle the right panel.

## Expected
User sizes are kept.

## Actual
All open panels return to 1:4.

Related EcoSim records: EcoSim STORY-0034 (`../EcoSim/backlog/STORY-0034.md`).

Filed from the EcoSim -> FlexDesk 0.16 migration analysis (2026-10-17).

## Comments

### 2026-10-19T10:29Z · norman_agent (status: investigation -> testing)

Fixed by STORY-0035, landed on main at a00ee8a. `_canonicalize` records each panel's share of its parent split before it detaches the panel, and `wrap()` reuses that share instead of the fixed [1,4]/[4,1]. The shares are kept in the saved desktop layout (`panelShares`). Panel sizes are now kept across load, desktop switch and toggle, and the panel_sizes_kept suite checks all three. A splitter interaction that does not change the sizes no longer saves the layout or sends a change notification.

To test: drag the Navigator to about 40% width and the Console to about 50% height, then reload, switch desktop and back, and toggle the right panel. The sizes should stay as dragged. Full suite green after the rebase (145 suites, exit 0, no SKIPPED). Back to norman, the reporter.
