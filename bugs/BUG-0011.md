## Description

src/ui/components/tree_view.js:294-302 decides category visibility only from .twm-tree-node descendants.

## Steps to reproduce
1. createTreeCategory, append createTreeItem rows directly.
2. filterTree(container, '') or with a matching query.

## Expected
The category stays visible with its items.

## Actual
The category gets display:none for every filter value, including clearing it.
