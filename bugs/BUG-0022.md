## Description

plot_popout_window.js:890/895 call window.pywebview.api.hp_filter; :653/658 hard-code 'streaming:delta'/'streaming:complete'; gallery_picker.js:66-68 calls window.pywebview.api.gallery_list; toast.js:17-18 reads window.__ecoagent.eventBus. host.js:10 says hosts are always injected.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Use FlexDesk outside pywebview with an injected host and bus; enable HP trend, call toastError, mount the gallery picker without entries.

## Expected
Everything comes from the injected host, services or bus.

## Actual
The features silently do nothing.
