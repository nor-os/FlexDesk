## Description

Found by EcoSim STORY-0037 (`../EcoSim/backlog/STORY-0037.md`) on FlexDesk 0.17.1. NotificationCenter gives toast cards the unprefixed classes `notification` and `notification--<severity>` (dist/widgets.js around 7216; the summary card at 7088 uses `notification twm-notification--summary`). flexdesk.css has no rule for those classes and no `@keyframes notification-in`, although a rule refers to that animation. Toasts therefore have no background and no severity border, and they inherit `pointer-events: none` from the root, so their buttons cannot be clicked.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Mount NotificationCenter and show a warn toast with an action button.
2. Look at the toast and try to click its button.

## Expected

Toast cards carry prefixed classes that flexdesk.css styles (background, severity border, entry animation defined in the sheet), receive pointer events, and their buttons work. A regression test reads the classes and the sheet. Ships in 0.17.2.

A new `@keyframes twm-notification-in` is defined. The notification-history panel used the undefined `notification-in` and now points at the new keyframes.
- **Tests:** tests/notification_progress.test.mjs now expects the prefixed severity classes. The new regression suite tests/notification_toast_styles.test.mjs has 43 failing assertions against origin/main's source and both stylesheets, and passes with the fix. It covers only-twm- classes on toast, summary and live region; jsdom computed styles from each sheet (root `pointer-events: none`, card `auto`, background, warn border); each sheet's rules and order, and that every animation a notification rule uses has a `@keyframes` in that sheet; the action button calling its handler, and a click on the card closing it.
