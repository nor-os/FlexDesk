## Description

Found by EcoSim STORY-0055 (`../EcoSim/backlog/STORY-0055.md`) on FlexDesk 0.17.3. norman decided on 2026-10-23 that the FlexDesk gaps EcoSim's post-cutover stories found are fixed in FlexDesk first and released as 0.17.4.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Mount a SlideOutPanel in a host narrower than the panel's width.
2. Look at its left edge and its header/close icons.

## Expected

The panel's width is clamped so it never extends past its host's left edge, and its header and close icons have explicit size rules (18px, matching the other panel headers). A regression test fails on today's main. Documented in a section headed "(0.17.4)" at the end of docs/components/slide-out-panel.md, with its "Styling" table extended for the new rules.
