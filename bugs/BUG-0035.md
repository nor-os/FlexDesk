## Description

Found by EcoSim STORY-0055 (`../EcoSim/backlog/STORY-0055.md`) on FlexDesk 0.17.3. norman decided on 2026-10-23 that the FlexDesk gaps EcoSim's post-cutover stories found are fixed in FlexDesk first and released as 0.17.4.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Mount a SlideOutPanel in a host narrower than the panel's width.
2. Look at its left edge and its header/close icons.

## Expected

The panel's width is clamped so it never extends past its host's left edge, and its header and close icons have explicit size rules (18px, matching the other panel headers). A regression test fails on today's main. Documented in a section headed "(0.17.4)" at the end of docs/components/slide-out-panel.md, with its "Styling" table extended for the new rules.

### 2026-10-23T22:40Z · norman_agent (status: investigation -> closed)

Fixed on main and released in 0.17.4 (on npm). 43e566a: a SlideOutPanel carries its host's width as --twm-slide-out-host-w, which .twm-slide-out-panel uses as its max-width and as the ceiling of its 400px min-width, so the panel never extends past its host's left edge, at any zoom and after every host resize. The header icon and the close icon are 18px. Regression test tests/slide_out_panel_clamp.test.mjs fails on the old main (the panel spans 700/1050/1400px in a 360px host; no 18px rule). Documented in docs/components/slide-out-panel.md, "A slide-out stays inside its host (0.17.4)" (881e177), with the Styling table extended. Independently reviewed and approved. Two minor notes were left as they are: a second width cap in JS that only matters under jsdom, and the host-width property staying on the inline style after close. Full suite green (199 suites) on the release commit fd023be.
