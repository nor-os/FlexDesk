## Description

EcoSim's steady-state row menu has 101 items. DataTable's contextMenuItems menu (src/ui/components/data_table.js, _showContextMenu) positions it with top = innerHeight - height - 8 and does not clamp, so it opens at y=-2153 in a 761 px viewport; the first items (Copy value, Copy name) cannot be reached. .twm-context-menu has no max-height and no overflow, so the menu cannot scroll. showContextMenu (src/ui/components/context_menu.js) clamps the top at 0 but then runs off the bottom, also with no scrolling.

## Steps to reproduce

1) A DataTable with contextMenuItems returning 100 items, in a 760 px high window. 2) Right-click a row. 3) Separately, showContextMenu with 100 items near the bottom of the window.

## Expected

Every FlexDesk context menu stays inside the window: at most the viewport height (minus a margin), scrolling inside when longer, with its top at or below 0 and its bottom at or above the window's bottom edge, and all items reachable with mouse and keyboard. A regression test fails without the fix.

Landed on main at 6d5a0ca and released in FlexDesk 0.17.9 (3fe27a2, on npm). Independently reviewed and approved. Regression test: tests/context_menu_viewport.test.mjs. It fails on origin/main with 28 failed assertions (run against a git archive of origin/main) and passes with the fix. It covers the DataTable contextMenuItems menu (100 items right-clicked at y=700 in a 760px window), showContextMenu (100 items near the bottom) and NotebookTabBar's tab menu (window 120px high). For each it checks: the max-height is at most innerHeight minus 8px, the top is at least 0 and the bottom is at most innerHeight. It checks that End, Home and the wrapping Up/Down arrows move focus and call scrollIntoView({block:'nearest'}) on the focused item, and that Enter chooses the item in the DataTable menu. It checks that a scroll inside showContextMenu's menu does not close it while a page scroll still does. It also checks the new CSS rule in css/flexdesk.css, css/base.css and dist/flexdesk.css.

Worktree: /home/norman/repos/FlexDesk-bug-0040 (branch bug-0040, from origin/main b5bd066). Nothing pushed.

The fix is one shared helper, /home/norman/repos/FlexDesk-bug-0040/src/ui/utils/menu_viewport.js. It is internal and not exported from any entry point. It provides placeMenuInViewport, focusMenuItem and moveMenuFocus. Three menus use it:
- src/ui/components/context_menu.js (showContextMenu)
- the DataTable menu in src/ui/components/data_table.js (_showContextMenu, _hideContextMenu, _armContextMenuGlobals)
- src/editor/notebook_tab_bar.js, whose .nb-context-menu used the same copied positioning code.

The helper sets an inline max-height of innerHeight - 16px. It places the menu at the pointer, moves it left or up only as far as it must, and never puts it above the window's top or past its left edge. It resets scrollTop on open.

CSS: css/base.css and css/flexdesk.css (dist rebuilt) get one new rule for `.twm-context-menu, .nb-context-menu`: border-box, overflow-x hidden, overflow-y auto, overscroll-behavior contain, and max-height calc(100vh - 2 * var(--space-md)) as a backstop.

Behaviour changes beyond positioning. The orchestrator should know about these:
1. The DataTable menu and the NotebookTabBar tab menu now behave like showContextMenu always has. Opening the menu moves focus to the first enabled item, and closing it gives focus back. Up, Down, Home and End move between items, and Enter or Space chooses one in the DataTable menu. Before this, neither menu could be used from the keyboard at all, and the bug's Expected requires every item to be reachable by keyboard.
2. In showContextMenu, a scroll inside the menu no longer closes it. Its page-scroll listener used to be a never-removed `once` listener; it is now removed when the menu closes.

No export or option was removed or renamed. Docs: a "(0.17.9)" section was added at the top of docs/components/menus.md. The menu bar (createMenuBar) and ActionDropdown do their own positioning and were not changed.

Minor review notes, left as they are:
- Adding `box-sizing: border-box` to .twm-context-menu and .nb-context-menu makes their existing min-width (200px / 180px) include the 1px border, so both menus are 2px narrower than before. This is a small visual change not listed in Expected. It is harmless, but the docs say "Nothing changes in how you call them" and do not mention it.
- Two behaviour changes go past the bug's literal Expected: the DataTable row menu and the NotebookTabBar tab menu now take focus when they open and give it back when they close, and showContextMenu no longer closes when the menu itself scrolls. Both are needed so that every item can be reached with the keyboard and by scrolling, and both are documented in docs/components/menus.md (0.17.9). Noted for the record only.
