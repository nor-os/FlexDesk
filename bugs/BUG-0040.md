## Description

EcoSim's steady-state row menu has 101 items. DataTable's contextMenuItems menu (src/ui/components/data_table.js, _showContextMenu) positions it with top = innerHeight - height - 8 and does not clamp, so it opens at y=-2153 in a 761 px viewport; the first items (Copy value, Copy name) cannot be reached. .twm-context-menu has no max-height and no overflow, so the menu cannot scroll. showContextMenu (src/ui/components/context_menu.js) clamps the top at 0 but then runs off the bottom, also with no scrolling.

## Steps to reproduce

1) A DataTable with contextMenuItems returning 100 items, in a 760 px high window. 2) Right-click a row. 3) Separately, showContextMenu with 100 items near the bottom of the window.

## Expected

Every FlexDesk context menu stays inside the window: at most the viewport height (minus a margin), scrolling inside when longer, with its top at or below 0 and its bottom at or above the window's bottom edge, and all items reachable with mouse and keyboard. A regression test fails without the fix.
