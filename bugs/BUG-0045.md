## Description

After BUG-0044 (0.17.10), setting the accent tokens rethemes #007acc, its tints and the light accent, but these accent-blue shades are still literals: the primary button hover (#1486cf, modal primary #1683c4), the flow-lanes ring (#1683c4, #5b9bd5, #0f6fa8, rgba(22,131,196)), the selected tile (#78b9ff, rgba(120,185,255)), the row cursor and snap preview (rgba(17,119,179)), #79b8f8, #9ecfff, the plot window and spinner (#4a90d9) and #268bd2. docs/concepts/styling.md lists them. None of them is an exact mix of an accent token, so deriving them would move their default colours.

## Steps to reproduce

Set `--accent`, `--accent-hover` and `--accent-bright` to magenta on :root and hover a primary button, select a tile, or look at the flow lanes.

## Expected

Owner decision: either derive these from the accent tokens too and accept small default colour shifts (as BUG-0044 did for #569cd6, about 1.5 ΔE), or settle on one accent (tokens.css: "Phase 5 will pick one") and fold the shades into it, or keep them as deliberate literals.
