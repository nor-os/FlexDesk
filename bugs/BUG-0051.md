## Description

norman, 2026-10-26 (EcoSim): "the fly-in config panel flies in from the right, crossing right panel. this should not be and needs fixing."

While open, the panel is `position: fixed` and pinned to its host's right edge (#trackGeometry), and it slides in from `translateX(100%)`: it starts outside the host, and a fixed element is not clipped by the host's `overflow: hidden`, so during the animation (and the close animation) it is drawn over the right dock panel or anything else right of the host.

## Expected

The panel appears from its host's right edge and is never drawn outside its host.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] While opening, open and closing, the panel is clipped to its host's rectangle (a fixed frame tracking the host's rect, `overflow: hidden`, inside which the panel slides); the backdrop stays the host's.
- [ ] Geometry tracking (scroll, resize, splitter, zoom) and the width cap work as before. Test; docs.
