## Description

Found in EcoSim on FlexDesk 0.19.0: the ETL pipeline progress window (a resizable `ManagedWindow` whose content is an `overflow-y: auto` list filling the window) shows no scrollbar. The content scrolls with the wheel, but `installAutoScrollbars` creates the bar and sets it to `display: none`. `barCovered()` samples the track's top, middle and bottom; at the bottom, `elementsFromPoint` returns the window's 12px `.twm-managed-window__resize--se` corner, which lies over the content. BUG-0036 made `barCovered()` skip WindowChromeController's `.twm-resize-handle` strips, but not a ManagedWindow's own handles. Every resizable managed window whose scrolling content reaches its bottom edge is affected.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Under `installAutoScrollbars(document.body)`, open a `ManagedWindow` with `canResize: true` whose content is a scrolling element filling the window, taller than it.
2. Hover the content.

## Expected

The overlay bar shows: `barCovered()` ignores `.twm-managed-window__resize` elements. It still hides a bar that a real overlay covers. A regression test fails on today's main. Documented in docs/components/layout-widgets.md next to BUG-0036's section.

## Actual

The bar is created and stays `display: none`.

Fixed on main for 0.19.1: `barCovered()` skips `.twm-managed-window__resize` as it skips `.twm-resize-handle` and the overlay layer's bars. Regression checks added to tests/autoscroll_resize_handle_not_cover.test.mjs (2 of its 3 new assertions fail on the old main). Documented in docs/components/layout-widgets.md, "A managed window's content keeps its bar (0.19.1)". Confirmed in EcoSim's real page: with the bottom handles out of hit-testing the bar shows.
