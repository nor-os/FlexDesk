## Description

Found in EcoSim on FlexDesk 0.21.1, on dashboards of plot tiles (a `renderPlotGrid` each) in cells 160 to 580 px wide. Plotly does not wrap text, so:

- A legend name longer than the plot area (EcoSim's sector names, "Business outside AI (firms, software, IT services, utilities)") runs past the cell's left and right edges and is cut off. A legend above the plot is centred on the plot area and starts a new row at its width, but one entry wider than that sticks out on both sides.
- A reference line's label starts at 1.5% of the plot area and runs right; a long one runs past the right edge.
- A `'right'` (or `'left'`) legend in a narrow cell: Plotly grows the right margin for it until the plot area has a negative width, and the subplot shows its legend and no plot.

Separately, `createChart` (and `updateChart`, `createChartDeferred`, so every `FigureTile`) merged the layout over `DARK_THEME_LAYOUT` shallowly: a figure's own `xaxis` or `yaxis` replaced the theme's, and the theme's axes had no `automargin`, so long tick labels were cut off unless every caller set it.

## Steps to reproduce

1. `renderPlotGrid` into a 270 px cell, a subplot with `legendPosition: 'top'` and a series labelled `Business outside AI (firms, software, IT services, utilities)`.
2. The same subplot with `legendPosition: 'right'` in a 163 px cell.
3. A reference line labelled `Paid-hours ceiling (AWHAETP record, 1.0113)` in a 163 px cell.

## Expected

Every text inside the cell: names and labels broken into lines that fit, the hover naming a series on one line; a plot in every subplot.

## Actual

The name and the label cut off at the cell's edges; in step 2 `_fullLayout._size.w` is negative and no trace is drawn.

## Comments

### 2026-11-26 · norman_agent (status: (none) -> closed)

Fixed on main for 0.22.0. The grid fits each figure to its cell at each draw (`fitFigure` in src/charts/plot_grid.js): legend names broken with the new `wrapChartLabel` (exported from `@flexdesk/charts`, with `longestChartLine`) to the plot area's width above or below the plot, a third of the cell beside it, a wrapped trace given a `hovertemplate` with its name on one line in `<extra>`; a legend beside the plot drawn above it where 12 characters do not fit in a third of the cell; labels without an arrow broken to the room from their anchor to the figure's edge; a resize that changes any of it draws the figure again. `createChart` merges `xaxis` and `yaxis` one level deep over the theme's, whose axes carry `automargin: true`. Checked in EcoSim on the ai_economy project, scenario ai_boom: every `<text>` of every chart on its 13 dashboards inside its `.js-plotly-plot`, and every grid figure with a plot area, at 1600 and 1100 px windows. Regression checks in tests/plot_grid.test.mjs §9 and tests/figure_tile.test.mjs §8. Documented in docs/components/charts.md. (`legend.xref: 'container'` was tried for the legend above the plot and dropped: with `yref: 'paper'`, Plotly 2.35 reserves the right margin from the legend's left edge, which leaves no plot.)
